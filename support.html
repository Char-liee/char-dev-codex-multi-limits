Support and setup

A Mac companion is required to collect Codex log-based token statistics. The app and widgets support English and Korean and follow your device language.

Contact Char Dev: jonghojo1998@gmail.com. Public distribution is still being prepared.

Connect your devices

  1. Use macOS 14 or later on Mac, or iOS 17 or later on iPhone. The app is not optimized for iPad.
  2. For account limits, open Settings → Account management and add an account. Authentication opens OpenAI in your system browser.
  3. On Mac, open Settings → Token statistics settings and choose the .codex folder containing sessions and archived_sessions.
  4. To view token statistics on iPhone or combine multiple Macs, turn on iCloud token-statistics sync on each device using the same Apple account.
  5. Use Full sync to refresh. Account/settings sync and token-statistics sync are separate options.

Common questions

Why are my iPhone token totals empty?

The iPhone cannot read your Mac's Codex files directly. Connect the log folder on Mac, enable token-statistics sync on both devices, and refresh while the Mac app is running.

Why does the widget show an older value?

The app requests widget updates, but Apple controls their final timing. Refresh the app and check the account query or token aggregation time. A sleeping Mac cannot upload new local records.

Is API-equivalent cost my actual bill?

No. It is an estimate from recorded tokens and standard model prices. Subscription pricing, taxes, tools, long-context surcharges, and speed/service-tier premiums are excluded. Unknown models appear in tokens but not cost until you assign a price.

Why are some dates missing?

Only available Codex log records are counted. Web-only usage, deleted source logs, and other Macs that have not synced can be missing. A blank day means no recorded data, not proof that you did not use Codex.

Does choosing an activity account filter token totals?

No. Token totals and the daily chart combine all Macs and accounts. The account selector on the activity screen filters only the weekly-limit activity heatmap.

What happens when I turn off sync or remove an account?

Turning off sync stops synchronization but does not delete existing caches or cloud records. Removing an account does not erase global token statistics or your OpenAI account. See the retention and deletion details.
